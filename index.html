Root Causes

Common AC problems across Texas

The state's extreme heat and humidity make certain issues show up far more often here than elsewhere.

Refrigerant Leaks

Low refrigerant levels reduce cooling capacity significantly.

Clogged Filters

Dust and debris buildup restricts airflow through the system.

Frozen Evaporator Coils

Usually caused by poor airflow or low refrigerant levels.

Faulty Capacitors or Motors

Wear and tear from continuous heavy use during peak summer.

Thermostat Malfunctions

Incorrect readings or lost connectivity to the cooling system.

Drainage Blockages

Often the hidden cause behind unexpected water leaks.

Quick Checks

What to try before calling for emergency repair

Before you make that emergency call, a few basic checks take less than five minutes.

  • Verify thermostat settings — correct mode and temperature
  • Check the circuit breaker to see if it has tripped
  • Inspect the air filter — replace or clean if excessively dirty
  • Check the outdoor unit for debris or blockages around it
  • Check vents to ensure nothing is blocking airflow
